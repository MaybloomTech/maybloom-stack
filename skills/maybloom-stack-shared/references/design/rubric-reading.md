# Rubric: reading pages

For a page someone reads: documentation, an article, a landing page, a
help centre. Same marking as [rubric-app.md](./rubric-app.md): **probe**
where the capture script checks it, **judged** otherwise.

1. **Structure comes from the category.** Look at what real sites of the
   same kind do (a docs page: a left tree, a measure, a right outline) and
   begin there; depart only for a reason the idea supplies. **judged**
2. **One idea per screen.** Each screen of scrolling has one thing worth
   looking at and a reason for being there. A heading and a line adrift in
   empty space is unfinished, not minimal. **judged**
3. **Measure and scale.** Body text runs 60 to 75 characters a line, at 16
   or more, with a line height of about 1.6 to 1.75. Headings come from a
   fixed scale and step one level at a time. **judged**
4. **No eyebrow labels** above headings, and no tracked-capital labels in
   place of structure. **judged**
5. **Headings say something.** Read the headings alone: they tell this
   subject's story, and none would pass unchanged on a competitor's page.
   **judged**
6. **No invented figures,** testimonials or logos. What the page cannot
   show, it omits. **judged**
7. **Imagery is the thing itself:** the product working, the place, the
   object. A picture for mood alone goes; a drawn illustration where the
   product should be goes. **judged**
8. **Text contrast** of at least 4.5:1 (3:1 for large text, 24px or
   18.66px bold). **probe** (contrast)
9. **No horizontal overflow** at 390, 768 and 1280. **probe** (overflow)
10. **Keyboard-only focus ring** on links and controls. **probe** (focus)
11. **Nothing nested interactive, no console warnings.** **probe** (nested,
    console)
12. **Every link is a link:** destinations that exist, link text that says
    where it goes, never "click here". **judged**
